# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful, keep criticism focused on the work, protect private employment information, and assume good faith while maintaining clear technical standards.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, or sustained disruption. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
